# Privacy Policy — Style Spy (Web Edition)

**Style Spy – Web Edition** runs 100% locally inside your browser.

- **No External Servers**: Uploaded screenshots, pasted HTML/CSS, and exported stylesheets never leave your device.
- **No Tracking or Analytics**: Zero telemetry, cookies, or third-party CDNs.
- **Local Storage Only**: Saved Design Kits are stored in your browser's `localStorage` and can be erased anytime via **Clear Local Data**.
